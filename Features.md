iOS — Reduced Experience

iOS should be presented as a different capability tier, rather than promising Android-equivalent functionality.

Possible features:

VPN/network monitoring.
Connection status.
Aggregate traffic statistics.
Historical network usage generated by your own extension.
Network diagnostics.
Local usage history.
Alerts based on information available to the extension.

Avoid promising:

"See exactly how much data Instagram used."

Instead, clearly explain that iOS imposes restrictions on identifying traffic generated by other apps, so the iOS experience will have less granular per-app attribution.
